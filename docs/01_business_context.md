# Business Context

**Status:** COMPLETED

## Purpose
Document the overall business context of the project.

## Questions this document will answer
- Why are we building this?
- Who is the target user?
- What are the business drivers?

## Findings

### Why are we building this?
The financial operations landscape is shifting. While traditional automation (like RPA and basic OCR) successfully handles "happy path" transactions, it completely fails on exceptions. Exceptions require human analysts to perform "detective work"—gathering context from ERPs, cross-referencing policies, and making judgment calls. This is expensive and slow.
We are building the **Agent-to-Agent Financial Collaboration Platform** to prove that modern AI can handle these complex exceptions autonomously by orchestrating specialized AI agents.

### The Target User
1. **Accounts Receivable / Accounts Payable Analysts:** Will spend less time manually investigating exceptions and more time reviewing high-level AI recommendations.
2. **Treasury Professionals:** Will receive real-time, predictive cash flow updates based on AI investigations, rather than waiting for end-of-month reconciliation.
3. **Risk & Compliance Officers:** Will have fully auditable, policy-grounded AI decisions to review.

### Business Drivers
1. **Reduce Cost-per-Exception:** Moving exceptions from manual investigation to AI auto-resolution or AI-assisted review.
2. **Accelerate Cash Flow:** Faster resolution of AR exceptions means faster cash application and improved working capital.
3. **Modernize Tech Stack:** Transitioning from legacy batch processing to real-time, agentic, cloud-native architectures, aligning with Deluxe's stated strategic direction.
