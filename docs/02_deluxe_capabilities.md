# Deluxe Capabilities

**Status:** COMPLETED

## Purpose
Document Deluxe's existing capabilities relevant to financial operations, AI, payments, AR, AP, treasury, and agentic AI.

## Questions this document will answer
- What is Deluxe's current AI capability in AR/AP?
- What are the limitations of current solutions like Receivables360+?
- What is Deluxe's stated direction regarding Agentic AI?

## Findings

### Receivables360+ and Cash Application
Deluxe currently utilizes AI and machine learning extensively for data extraction (OCR) and intelligent matching. When unstructured remittance data arrives via email or PDF, Receivables360+ extracts the relevant data (invoice numbers, amounts) and attempts to match it against the ERP's open invoices. 
**Business Value:** This significantly reduces manual data entry and accelerates cash flow.
**Limitation:** It is primarily an "automation" tool, not an "autonomous" reasoning engine. When exceptions occur, they fall into manual queues.

### Treasury and Liquidity Management
Deluxe provides centralized dashboards to consolidate payment data and give treasury teams visibility into liquidity.
**Business Value:** Better cash flow management and working capital optimization.
**Direction:** Deluxe leadership has publicly stated a strategic focus on integrating "agentic" and generative AI as digital copilots for treasury professionals.

### The Opportunity
While Deluxe has solved the basic AI data extraction and matching problem, there is a clear gap in handling **complex financial exceptions**. The opportunity is to build a multi-agent system where specialized agents (Payment, AR, Risk, Treasury, Policy) collaborate to investigate exceptions, determine root causes, and recommend resolutions autonomously, moving from "automation" to "autonomy."
