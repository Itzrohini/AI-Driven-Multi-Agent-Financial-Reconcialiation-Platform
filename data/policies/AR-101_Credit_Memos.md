# Global Policy: AR-101 Credit Memo Application

## Overview
This policy defines the rules for applying open credit memos to outstanding invoices during the cash application process.

## Rules
1. **Automatic Application Threshold**: If a customer remits a partial payment (short pay) on an open invoice, and the remaining balance exactly matches an available open credit memo on the customer's account, the system may automatically apply the credit memo to close the invoice.
2. **Value Limit**: Automatic credit memo application is strictly limited to short pays of $5,000.00 USD or less.
3. **Approval Requirement**: If the short pay exceeds $5,000.00 USD, a human analyst must manually review the remittance advice to ensure the customer intended to use the credit memo.
