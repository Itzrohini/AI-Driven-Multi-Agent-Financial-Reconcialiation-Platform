# Master Service Agreement - Globex Inc

## Section 4. Payment Terms
All invoices are due Net 30. 

## Section 4.2. Purchase Orders
Globex Inc strictly requires a valid Purchase Order (PO) number on all invoices exceeding $10,000.00 USD. If an invoice exceeds this amount and lacks a PO, payment will be held until a PO is provided by the Globex procurement team.

## Section 7. Dispute Resolution
Any short pays related to SLAs must be documented in writing within 15 days of invoice receipt.
