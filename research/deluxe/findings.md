**Status:** COMPLETED

## Source 1
* Official source: Deluxe Corporation (deluxe.com)
* Product/capability: Receivables360+
* Business problem: Highly manual, slow, and error-prone accounts receivable and cash application processes.
* Existing AI capability: AI and machine learning for intelligent data extraction and matching.
* Technology/AI approach: Uses OCR and machine learning algorithms to scan unstructured remittance data from emails and PDFs, normalizing it and matching it to open invoices.
* Business value: Reduces manual data entry ("detective work"), accelerates cash flow, and lowers Days Sales Outstanding (DSO).
* Limitations/gaps: Current AI is focused on data extraction and basic matching. It does not natively investigate *why* a match failed (e.g., cross-referencing credit memos, analyzing risk, consulting policies) autonomously.
* Potential opportunity for our prototype: Build agentic workflows that handle the exceptions—when Receivables360+ fails to make a match, agents collaborate to investigate the root cause.
* URL: https://www.deluxe.com/paymentss/receivables/
* Date accessed: 2026-08-25

## Source 2
* Official source: Deluxe Corporation Tech Blogs / Executive Interviews
* Product/capability: Treasury and Liquidity Management
* Business problem: Lack of real-time visibility into cash flow and liquidity.
* Existing AI capability: Transitioning toward "agentic" and generative AI acting as digital copilots for treasury professionals.
* Technology/AI approach: Utilizing cloud-based data aggregation and LLMs for natural language interaction with treasury systems.
* Business value: Enables treasury professionals to gain tailored insights and execute actions more efficiently.
* Limitations/gaps: Early stages of agentic AI. Mostly functioning as "copilots" (assistants) rather than fully autonomous, collaborating agent systems that resolve complex exceptions.
* Potential opportunity for our prototype: A multi-agent system where a Treasury Agent calculates real-time cash flow impacts of AR exceptions and advises the Decision Agent, bridging the gap between Receivables and Treasury.
* URL: https://www.deluxe.com
* Date accessed: 2026-08-25
